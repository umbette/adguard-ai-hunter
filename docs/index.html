The RePlug™ Ecosystem

Specialized local modules designed to transform existing silicon into dependable household guardians without subscriptions or disposable hubs.

Module 01 • Available Now

RePlug Sentinel Shield

Network defense console bridging local DNS resolvers (AdGuard Home / Pi-hole) with Home Assistant to block tracking, telemetry, and predatory pricing.

  • 15 deterministic shields for Smart TVs, vacuums, 3D printers & cars
  • Anti-dynamic pricing protection against travel & hotel price gouging
  • Caregiver anti-phishing SMS alerts & Crypto RPC integrity vault
  • Zero-knowledge 1-click unbreak catalog & Scan-on-Demand audit
Module 02 • Core Flagship Design

RePlug Nozzle Guardian

Protects inkjet EcoTank (CISS) and 3D printer heads from drying out or failing unattended, operated entirely over local LAN via IPP and camera vision.

  • Idle-window state tracking via RFC 8011 local IPP protocol
  • Adaptive low-waste micro-purge before ink crystallization
  • On-device macro vision check for nozzle test & first-layer integrity
  • Thermal 50%–80% battery ceiling guard via Home Assistant smart plug
Module 03 • Roadmap Nodes

Ambient & Blackout Sentinels

Leverages built-in lithium battery backup, MEMS accelerometers, and microphones cleanly inside RAM for resilience when the grid drops.

  • Blackout Beacon: emergency LED trigger & freezer thaw warnings
  • Acoustic Sentry: local smoke/CO siren tonal recognition
  • Optical Meter OCR: periodic flash-assisted analog meter logging
  • Offline LAN emergency intercom during external ISP outages

The Anti-Obsolescence Manifesto

1. Zero New Plastic: If hardware already inside your home can handle the workload, selling you a new disposable dongle is environmental waste.

2. Dignity for Silicon: Hardware from 2018 possesses ten times the compute capability of modern commercial smart-home sensors. It deserves sovereign software, not a landfill.

3. Privacy Without Subscriptions: Domestic cameras, DNS logs, and microphones never belong on third-party ad-tech servers.
