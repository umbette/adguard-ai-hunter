The Anti-Obsolescence Manifesto

1. Zero New Plastic: If a device already in your drawer can perform the task, we will never ask you to buy another piece of disposable plastic.

2. Dignity for Silicon: A 2018 smartphone octa-core processor is ten times more capable than contemporary commercial IoT gadgets. It just needs the right local software.

3. Absolute Local Privacy: No surveillance, no cloud subscription traps, no behavioral ad profiling. Your data never leaves your home network.
